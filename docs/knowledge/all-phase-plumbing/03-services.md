## Water heaters

FACT: Common problems handled: no hot water, rusty water, leaking tanks, popping/rumbling noises (sediment). Services: tank and tankless installation/replacement, repair, annual flush, anode rod replacement, thermostat/heating element replacement, gas and electric units. Tank heaters last 8-12 years; tankless units are rated 20+ years with 25-35% energy savings but higher upfront cost. Same-day service for most jobs when booked before 2pm weekdays; 24/7 emergency water heater service available.

## Drain cleaning & hydro jetting

FACT: Handles slow-draining sinks/tubs/showers, gurgling drains, foul odors, recurring clogs. Standard drain cleaning uses augers/snakes for typical blockages; hydro jetting uses up to 4,000 PSI water pressure for severe grease buildup, scale, or tree roots when a plunger/snake can't clear it — used residentially and commercially. Camera inspection available to find leaks/cracks/root intrusion without digging.

## Sewer repair & replacement

FACT: Warning signs: slow drains, frequent clogs, foul sewer odors, gurgling toilets, water pooling in the yard. Repair fixes localized damage (traditional excavation or trenchless); replacement installs new piping, typically for severely collapsed/corroded lines — the company inspects with a camera first to decide which. Trenchless repair uses small access points at each end of the line to install a liner/replacement internally — less digging, faster, less landscaping cost. Trenchless typically 1-2 days; traditional excavation can take several days to a week.
SOURCE URL: https://www.allphaseplumbing.com/services/sewer-services/sewer-repair
VERIFIED: 2026-09-05 | STATUS: approved

## Toilets

FACT: Handles running toilets, weak/incomplete flushing, leaking seals/base leaks, faulty flapper/refill valves, cracked tank or bowl. Replacement is recommended after 2+ repairs in a year, structural tank/bowl damage, or an old high-water-consumption model — newer efficient units often pay back their cost in utility savings within a few years. Also does new installs (renovation/new construction, including smart toilets and bidet-integrated units).

## Sump pumps

FACT: Addresses basement flooding risk during heavy rain. Repair covers pumps that run constantly, fail to pump, have a stuck float, a frozen discharge line, or electrical issues. Replacement recommended for systems 7-10+ years old, including battery-backup options. Also does new installs (basin, check valve, drainage termination). Framed as a preventive-maintenance opportunity, not only a post-failure fix.
SOURCE URL: https://www.allphaseplumbing.com/services/plumbing/sump-pumps
VERIFIED: 2026-09-05 | STATUS: approved

## Repiping

FACT: Warning signs: rust-colored or metallic-tasting water, leaks appearing in multiple spots within a year, reduced pressure when multiple fixtures run at once, visible corrosion on exposed pipe, homes 50+ years old with original galvanized steel. Primarily uses PEX (flexible, freeze-tolerant, 50+ year rating); uses Type L copper when code or customer preference requires it. Most single-family homes take 2-5 days, done in phases to restore water service each evening where possible. Written estimate before work begins; permits/inspections coordinated by the company.
SOURCE URL: https://www.allphaseplumbing.com/services/plumbing/repiping
VERIFIED: 2026-09-05 | STATUS: approved

## Water softeners

FACT: Hard water causes mineral buildup that shortens fixture/appliance life; signs of a failing softener include soap scum/cloudy dishes/stiff laundry, low pressure from mineral buildup in pipes, salty or metallic-tasting water, or unusually high salt consumption. Services: installation, repair (salt bridges, resin, valves), and maintenance (salt refill, brine tank cleaning, resin/valve inspection) — annual maintenance recommended.
